Chapter I — The Ritual
i.

Dawn breaks over the city.

Before the traffic, before the noise, there's a small stall with a kettle that never rests.

ii.

The kettle begins to sing.

Loose leaves dance in boiling water, deepening from gold to a rich, dark amber.

iii.

Milk swirls in like a cloud.

Poured high, from cup to cup, until a soft foam crowns the glass. That's how you know it's right.
